# LEGAL INPUTS REQUIRED

Fill-in template. Every value below must come from the business owner —
none may be guessed or invented. When completed, these values feed
directly into [PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) and
[WEBSITE_TERMS_DRAFT.md](WEBSITE_TERMS_DRAFT.md), after which the pages
can be finalized, reviewed and published.

## What happens after these are provided

1. Placeholders in the two draft documents are replaced with the values
   above.
2. Drafts are reviewed (per items 15–16).
3. Public routes are created (`/privacy-policy`, `/terms`), added to the
   footer and the sitemap.
4. The quote-form privacy notice is added (see "Form privacy notice
   plan" in [LEGAL_REQUIREMENTS.md](LEGAL_REQUIREMENTS.md)).
